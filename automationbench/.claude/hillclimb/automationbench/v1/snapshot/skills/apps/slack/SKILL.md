This is the operating procedure for the Slack app (post messages, channels, users, search, etc.).

- Routing rules and process notes are often plain messages in a channel. Call slack_list_channels, then slack_list_channel_messages on the target channel and any ops/policy/rules channel before deciding how to route or what to post.
- In summaries include every required code/ID and the per-item names and amounts you acted on.
