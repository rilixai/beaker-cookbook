Retain **25 exclusions** with [AutomationBench-PI at `858718b19e`](https://github.com/xeophon/AutomationBench-PI/tree/858718b19e5d66c74451432783d34ee64a550a15). Its published dataset contains **800 tasks**. The filtered recipe loads **775 tasks: 584 train / 191 test**.

## Migration policy

The previous recipe excluded 122 tasks against Zapier commit `4a8e1061254004d9dac807054eed33fad7d1ff14`. PI directly changes 97 of those task definitions. This migration restores those 97 and retains the 25 unchanged definitions below. The comparison ignores formatting and docstrings and matches complete `domain.task_name` IDs. Shared tool/grader changes are not treated as direct task edits. Restoration follows this selection policy; it does not certify that every previous defect is resolved.

PI's audit discusses 806 task-builder functions, but its dataset loaders publish 800 tasks: six sales builders are not registered. The recipe uses the published loaders, including all 200 `simple` tasks, rather than loading dormant functions.
