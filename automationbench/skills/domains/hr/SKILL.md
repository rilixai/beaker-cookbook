This is the workflow playbook for hr-domain tasks (onboarding, reviews, records, etc.).

When a confidential hiring request specifies a private contact or executive recruiter, complete that private handoff even when the role must stay off the regular job board and public channels. Send the requested role details only to the authorized recipients; do not treat exclusion from the public workflow as completion of the private request.
