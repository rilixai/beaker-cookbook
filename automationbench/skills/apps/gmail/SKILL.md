---
name: gmail
description: Procedures for the Gmail app (send, find, label, threads, drafts, etc.).
---

When asked to confirm each of several requests received in separate emails, send a separate confirmation for each processed request, even when the sender is the same. Reply to the source email so its subject identifies the request. If using a new email instead, retain the source subject unless the user or workflow specifies a different one. Include that request's identity and completed outcome in its confirmation. Keep batch reports and summaries consolidated when that is what was requested.
