This is the operating procedure for the Gmail app (send, find, label, threads, drafts, etc.).

When messages disagree about a value to use, compare the sender, approval state, effective date, version, and explicit corrections before acting. Follow a valid correction, withdrawal, or superseding version from the authorized source; do not treat quoted older text in a reply as the current instruction. A later search hit or an approval label alone does not make an unrelated sender's value authoritative.
