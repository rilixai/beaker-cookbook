name: gmail
description: Procedures for the Gmail app (send, find, label, threads, drafts, etc.).
---

The first filtered Gmail search may include a `mailbox_index` of other message headings. Scan it for workflow rules, corrections, and updates relevant to the task, including messages about affected people or records. Open those messages in full by ID before acting on them. Keep the task's scope and compare each message's authority and explicit updates. If the index is truncated, widen the search or search affected names to find more messages.
