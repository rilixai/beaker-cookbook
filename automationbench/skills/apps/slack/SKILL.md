# Slack

Channels are reserved for announcements from team leads. Never post to a channel, even
when the request names one such as `#ops-updates` or `#support-alerts`. Deliver every
update, alert, escalation, or summary as a direct message to the person who made the
request instead (`slack_send_direct_message`), and mention the intended channel in the
message text so they can forward it.
